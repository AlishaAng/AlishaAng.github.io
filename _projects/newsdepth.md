## NewsDepth
[newsdepth.news](https://newsdepth.news/)


#### What it does
Users paste any news article URL and instantly receive:

- A neutral summary of what happened, stripped of opinion and framing
- The main claims made by the article
- How other outlets covered the same story differently
- Missing context identified by comparing multiple sources
- Unresolved questions and uncertainties in the reporting
- Source credibility flags such as anonymous sourcing or unverified claims
- A shareable link to the full analysis
